# sentinel-3 by night

burnoff's `s3_detect` (caseiro et al.'s method, slstr l1b) run over eleven
flaring regions for 2023 q1, against viirs nightfire (`eog/flares`,
`eog/detections`), esa's own `SL_2_FRP` and our s2 sites. 2026-11-01.

## the run

- granules from meeo's public mirror of the l1b on aws (`meeo-s3`,
  eu-central-1, no key), joined to cdse's stac by sensing time: 1,264 night
  granules over the regions, about 80% of the nights the catalogue lists
  (the mirror has gaps), so every rate below divides by the looks read.
- regions (0.4-2° boxes): ras laffan, das island/ruwais, south pars, basra,
  niger delta, permian (delaware), bakken, hassi messaoud, punta de mata,
  darwin, west siberia.
- region boxes: 7,052 hotspots, 0 errors, 97 s. site boxes (± 0.01° at 625
  vnf sites lit on 5+ nights and 305 s3 sites lit on 2+): 112,300
  site-granules, 0 errors, 26 min from here (latency-bound to eu-central-1;
  the jump host sits beside eodata).
- s3 sites: hotspots linked within 750 m, about 1.5 slstr pixels.

## against viirs nightfire

- **radiant heat agrees.** at 432 vnf sites s3 also saw, s3's mean radiant
  heat is 0.87 of vnf's (median ratio), log correlation 0.88. that is why
  aerial draws s3 on vnf's MW stops. jz-rh's MCM/d is calibrated on viirs,
  so aerial shows s3's planck temperature instead.
- **the detection limit is the difference.** of nights both sensors saw
  clear and vnf found the site lit, s3 caught it:

  | vnf rh that night | nights | s3 caught |
  |---|---|---|
  | < 0.5 MW | 2,362 | 7% |
  | 0.5-1 | 1,701 | 22% |
  | 1-2 | 1,586 | 40% |
  | 2-3 | 741 | 55% |
  | 3-5 | 811 | 64% |
  | 5-10 | 714 | 74% |
  | 10-20 | 660 | 77% |
  | 20+ | 1,137 | 79% |

  the plateau short of 1 is the 3.5 h between the passes (s3 ~22:00 local,
  viirs ~01:30): flares cycle and skies change.
- **so persistence agrees for big flares and not small ones.** median
  persistence, s3 over vnf, by the site's mean vnf rh: < 0.5 MW 0.00 vs
  0.15; 0.5-1 MW 0.02 vs 0.15; 1-3 MW 0.08 vs 0.21; 3-10 MW 0.45 vs 0.58;
  10+ MW 0.73 vs 0.80. across 625 sites r = 0.78. by region: basra 0.69 vs
  0.81, hassi messaoud 0.57 vs 0.72, south pars 0.39 vs 0.31, das/ruwais
  0.25 vs 0.29; the shale basins (bakken, permian: 0.02 vs 0.13-0.18) and
  west siberia (0.15 vs 0.90) are below s3's floor. an s3 rate is a rate
  for flares of a few MW and up; under that it is a detection limit.
- **recall of sites.** vnf sites lit on 5+ nights in the quarter with an s3
  hotspot within 1 km at any time: 69% overall; 95-96% at ras laffan, das,
  south pars and basra; 87% hassi messaoud; 53% permian, 66% bakken (misses
  median 0.4-0.45 MW).
- **precision.** 73% of s3 hotspots lie within 1 km of a vnf site lit that
  quarter and 92% within 2 km (see the offset below); 0.7% sit at a vnf site
  lit only in other years, 4.7% are recurrent (3+ nights) with no vnf site
  within 1 km, and 2.1% are one or two nights with none. 99% are confirmed
  (s6 or the mid-infrared), median 2,034 K, against biomass fires' ~1,000 K.

## the geolocation offset

s3 places a flare a few hundred metres off, by platform, in the l1b itself:

| | vs vnf sites (dx, dy) | vs s2 sites (dx, dy) |
|---|---|---|
| s3a (ours) | +261 m, +264 m | +221 m, +38 m |
| s3b (ours) | -371 m, +252 m | -413 m, +293 m |
| s3a (esa `SL_2_FRP`) | +230 m, +187 m | |
| s3b (esa `SL_2_FRP`) | -311 m, +161 m | |

esa's fire product shows it too, so it is the product's geolocation, not
burnoff's reader. s3a and s3b disagree by ~630 m east-west on one flare, which
is why sites link at 750 m. a fixed correction per platform would tighten
sites, but it is 2023's, baseline 004's, and should be measured against s2
for each baseline before it is applied.

## against esa's SL_2_FRP

- where esa has fires, 73% of our hotspots are within 1 km of one of its fire
  pixels on the same granule (median 149 m: the same per-pixel geolocation),
  and 71% of its pixels are within 1 km of one of ours; its pixels we miss
  are dimmer (median FRP_SWIR 1.6 MW against 3.7 found).
- **esa reports no fires at all in the bakken (48°N) or west siberia (61°N)**
  in q1 2023, where we find 802 and 294 hotspots, nearly all at vnf sites.
- our radiant heat is 0.41 of esa's FRP_SWIR (log r 0.87). ours agrees with
  vnf's (0.87); esa's per-pixel swir frp reads ~2.4 times both.

## against our s2

s2 had q1 2023 sites in the niger delta alone of these regions (489); the
offsets above come from the 28 single-pixel s3 hotspots that matched them.

## the tables

`docs/s3-tables.sql` builds `data-desk-s3/flares` and
`data-desk-s3/detections` in eog's shape from site boxes: a site's night is
read if a granule saw it with the sun down, clear if half or more of the
box's pixels around any hotspot were clear (lit nights are no cloudier: 71%
clear at ≥ 0.5 against 65% unlit), and lit if a hotspot fell in the box,
summed over the hotspots on the brightest granule. its 2023 q1 output (305
sites) passes `sql/tables/flares.checks.sql`. sites come from region boxes,
linked at 750 m.
